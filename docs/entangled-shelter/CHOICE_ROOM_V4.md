# Choice Room V4 — Production Contract

## Gallery

- Twenty possibilities per category, ten visible per edit.
- Four thematic filters plus All and My Possibilities.
- Independent illustrated artwork and exact copy for every preset.
- Persistent chosen tray across filter and edit changes.

## Games

### Fortune Deck

A salon table with a dimensional card, ghost deck, red/black call, and physical flip reveal.

### A Fair Fight

A symmetrical hand arena for rock, paper, scissors with a count-in and explicit opponent.

### Trust Your Hunch

A correspondence room where possibilities become sealed letters and the winning answer arrives as opened mail.

## Result rules

The random result is decided before decorative animation begins. Only a win may create an accepted decision; a tie or loss always returns the player to another round.
