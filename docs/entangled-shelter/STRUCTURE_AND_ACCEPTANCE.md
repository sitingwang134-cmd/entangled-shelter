# Entangled Shelter — Structure and Acceptance

## Product structure

The experience is a single editorial journey with five principal states:

1. Hero: establishes the Indecision Edition and the three suits.
2. Introduction: explains that chance supports desire rather than replacing it.
3. Taste, Style, and Wander chapters: each supplies twenty illustrated possibilities and a dedicated route into the decision room.
4. The Ritual: introduces three distinct games—Fortune Deck, A Fair Fight, and Trust Your Hunch.
5. The Decision Archive: preserves accepted answers in a living three-dimensional paper roll.

## Choice-room acceptance

- Each category contains exactly twenty named possibilities.
- The gallery reveals ten possibilities per edit and offers an explicit “Another Edit” control.
- Every possibility has its own name, description, and illustration.
- A user must select at least two possibilities before play.
- Personal possibilities may be added without clearing existing choices.
- Changing gallery edits never clears the chosen set.
- Each game presents a visually and mechanically distinct interface.
- A losing or tied round cannot be accepted.
- Accepted decisions are stored locally and remain available in the Decision Archive.

## Motion acceptance

- Scroll-linked chapter movement retains the original editorial rhythm.
- Main imagery uses layered depth rather than flat zoom alone.
- Wander includes a pointer- and keyboard-operable royal porthole.
- The porthole shade moves continuously during drag and settles after release.
- Reduced-motion preference preserves function while suppressing long transitions.

## Accessibility acceptance

- All interactive elements are keyboard reachable.
- The choice room is a modal dialog with focus containment and an Escape path.
- Visual state is paired with text or ARIA state.
- Generated imagery has descriptive alternative text or is explicitly decorative.
- Text and controls maintain readable contrast at desktop and mobile widths.
