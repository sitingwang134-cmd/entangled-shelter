# Output Plan — Travel Porthole Adaptation

Target: `https://www.mikes.cv/`

Scope: reproduce the interaction grammar of the reference window inside the existing Wander chapter, not the surrounding portfolio. The result must remain unmistakably part of Entangled Shelter.

## Design direction

- Product goal: turn the Wander chapter into a tactile invitation to leave the familiar.
- Tone: aristocratic, dreamlike, cinematic, and gently playful.
- Composition: a large vertical window paired with a short editorial caption inside the existing story card.
- Typography: retain Neue Montreal for utility language and the site's script/serif voices for romance.
- Surfaces: ivory faux fur, pearl silk, antique gold, deep bottle green, and a luminous landscape.
- Motion: vertical drag, responsive shade settling, restrained inner-scene parallax, and floating glints.
- Signature: a palace-carriage porthole whose soft white frame makes travel feel touchable.
