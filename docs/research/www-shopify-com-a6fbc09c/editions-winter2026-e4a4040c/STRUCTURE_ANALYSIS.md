# Structural Analysis — Shopify Editions Winter 2026

## Purpose

This reference was studied as an editorial motion system: full-viewport chapters, a persistent navigation rail, oversized typography, foreground cards, depth-led imagery, and scroll-linked scene transitions.

## Adapted principles

- A sticky cinematic background provides continuity while chapter content advances above it.
- Large chapter titles establish rhythm and orientation.
- Information alternates between dense editorial cards and open image-led scenes.
- Fine geometric lines give depth without competing with photography.
- Repeated micro-labels and folio numbers make the long page feel deliberately sequenced.
- Calls to action remain visible but secondary to narrative movement.

## Local interpretation

Entangled Shelter applies those principles to decision making rather than commerce. Taste, Style, and Wander are independent editorial rooms; the games provide the product mechanism; the archive turns completed decisions into a lasting visual record.

No original Shopify code or private assets are required by the production build.
