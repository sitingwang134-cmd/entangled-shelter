# Design Direction

1. Product goal: reproduce Shopify Editions Winter '26 as an editorial launch experience for commerce product updates.
2. Tone: exuberant, theatrical, optimistic.
3. Layout: full-viewport opening tableau followed by long vertical story chapters with oversized titles and isolated product panels.
4. Typography: monumental display lettering paired with compact utilitarian navigation and restrained body copy.
5. Color: warm parchment foundations, near-black typography, acid Shopify green, and chapter-specific saturated accents.
6. Motion: scroll-led reveals, floating decorative ornaments, slow depth shifts, and crisp hover responses.
7. Signature differentiator: a Renaissance-poster hero that behaves like a stage curtain opening into twelve color-coded product chapters.
