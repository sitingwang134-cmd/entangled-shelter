# Travel Porthole Specification

## Visual contract

- Outer frame reads as soft ivory fur rather than moulded plastic.
- Inner bezel is warm antique gold with visible highlight and depth.
- Viewport is tall, rounded, and clearly distinct from the outer frame.
- The generated landscape fills the complete opening without text or embedded framing.
- The pearl shade has folds, a weighted rail, and a hanging tassel.
- Deep green surroundings preserve contrast with the white frame and the broader Wander chapter.

## Interaction contract

- Pointer drag maps vertical distance continuously to a 0–100 open value.
- Release snaps at the midpoint.
- The viewport uses `role="slider"` with numeric and textual state.
- Keyboard controls: Arrow Up/Down, Home/End, Space/Enter.
- The exploration action remains an independent button.

## Responsive contract

- Two-column editorial arrangement at desktop widths.
- Single-column arrangement below 850 px.
- Window remains at least 240 px wide and preserves its aspect ratio.
